Privacy Policy

Infinity Fall

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Infinity Fall collects no personal data — none at all.

What the app stores

Your progress — score, pollen, unlocked levels and characters, records and settings — is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and it is removed when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email address.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs — the app has no external dependencies at all.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • Nothing is shared with, or sold to, anybody.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your leaderboard scores and achievements under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app sends Apple only your score, for the leaderboard. Game Center is entirely optional and the game is fully playable without it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

Any future change to this policy will be published on this page, with the date above updated.
