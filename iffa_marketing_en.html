Infinity Fall

Tilt. Dodge. Keep falling.

For iPhone

You're a little fruit tumbling through an endless sky — and there's no stopping. Tilt your phone to steer, slip past the obstacles and scoop up every grain of pollen on the way down. How far can you fall?

How to play

1

Tilt

Lean your iPhone left and right to steer — or drag with your finger if you prefer.

2

Dodge

Weave through spinning obstacles. The longer you survive, the faster you fall.

3

Collect

Grab pollen for points, then spend it on new characters and abilities.

What's inside

♾️

Infinity mode

An endless, ever-changing fall. Chase your best score, one run at a time.

🗺️

10 hand-crafted levels

Story levels with checkpoints, a finish line, and records to beat.

⭐

Hidden stars

Hunt down the stars tucked into every level to unlock a secret bonus stage.

🍎

Six characters

Start with three fruity friends and unlock the rest with your pollen.

⚡

Power-ups

Unlock Speed Boost, Ghost and Destroyer abilities to fall further.

🏆

Game Center

Climb the leaderboard and see how your score stacks up.
